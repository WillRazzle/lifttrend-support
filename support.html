LiftTrend Support

Beta support

Open TestFlight, select LiftTrend, and choose Send Beta Feedback.

What to include

Include the LiftTrend version and build from Review → About LiftTrend, your iPhone model and iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; review them for personal workout information before sharing.

If your data will not open

Keep the app installed. Close and reopen it. If the issue continues, send beta feedback before deleting anything; deleting the app removes its local data.

Using LiftTrend

Train is where you start and record workouts. History contains past sessions. Review shows training observations, preferences, evidence, and About LiftTrend.

Privacy

Read the LiftTrend privacy policy for information about local storage, Apple services, and beta feedback.
