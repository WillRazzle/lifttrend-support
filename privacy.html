LiftTrend Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

This policy describes LiftTrend version 0.15.1. LiftTrend helps you plan routines, record workouts, and review your training.

Data on your device

Workout records, routines, equipment profiles, and preferences are stored locally on your device. LiftTrend has no developer-operated server, user account, advertising, tracking, or analytics service. The app does not transmit your workout data to the developer.

Microphone and interpretation

This release does not use the microphone or speech recognition. Apple-assisted wording interpretation is disabled in this release.

Apple services and external links

iOS Live Activities can display your current workout on the Lock Screen. Your Apple device and backup settings may include app data. Apple may share your tester identity, crash and usage information, and submitted feedback with the developer through TestFlight. Apple handles this beta information under its own privacy policy and terms. Opening a research or exercise-reference link takes you to an external website, whose own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can edit saved training in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data. Backups may retain earlier copies according to your device and Apple settings. Before deleting the app to troubleshoot, send beta feedback so the issue can be investigated.

This website

These static support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or workout-data uploads.

Questions

During the beta, open TestFlight, select LiftTrend, and choose Send Beta Feedback. Review any screenshots and include only information needed to explain your question.
